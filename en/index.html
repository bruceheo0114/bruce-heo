About

Context Designer,
Bruce Heo

I believe campaigns begin with context, not messages. I first ask why people move—and where they hesitate—then design the language and experience that belong in that moment.

I have led 360° IMC campaigns across F&B, beauty, wellness, pharmaceutical, OTC, and health supplement brands—connecting content, KOLs, performance, media, and offline experiences into one customer journey, then measuring the result.

At PR ONE, my remit has extended beyond brand campaigns to integrated PR, media relations, ESG, and internal communications—shaping how brand and corporate messages reach consumers, media, and employees in the right context.

What I notice in the field becomes marketing essays and my Brunch Book, The Work of Designing Context. Observation, interpretation, and writing are extensions of the work itself.

I also teach marketers how to move beyond raw AI output and structure campaign proposals that connect objectives, insight, message, execution, and KPIs.

What I do

The practice of context design
moves through four steps

01
Read

Find the consumer context a brand has missed by studying what Gen X, Millennials, and Gen Z notice and how they respond.

02
Design

Design 360° IMC campaigns and customer journeys around the context—message, tone, and timing together.

03
Connect

Connect content, KOLs, performance, media, and offline experiences into one flow across F&B, beauty, wellness, and healthcare.

04
Prove

1M+ organic views on a single post, +400% YouTube, and +200% Instagram. Not intuition alone—numbers.
